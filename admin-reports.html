User Management

Manage staff access to the health-center system. Staff accounts are separate from patient records.

Invite a staff member

Send a sign-up invitation to a staff member’s work email. This creates a staff authentication account, not a patient record.

Designations label staff accounts; they do not yet grant different page permissions. Invitation access is checked server-side against Supabase-managed app metadata, which users cannot edit themselves. For first-time setup, the project owner must set the authorized administrator’s Auth user app_metadata role to Administrator.
